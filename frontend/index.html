About Sentock

Sentock is a lightweight financial news sentiment analyzer engineered to convert textual market noise into actionable quantitative signals. By combining transformer models (FinBERT) with domain-tuned classifiers (ReyZer) and rule-based lexicon models (VADER, TextBlob), Sentock provides robust sentiment insights without single-model bias.
